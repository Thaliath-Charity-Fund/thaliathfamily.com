The Thaliath Charity Fund

Founded by our forefathers in 1954 for the unity and welfare of the family and the people of Varapuzha

Having thus founded the family on the rock bed of power and pluck, the forefathers of our family established a charity fund in 1954 for the unity and welfare of the entire family and others as well. Each member of the family made a monthly contribution to the fund, thereby raising sufficient capital for the Thaliath Charity Fund. The activities of the fund then began to flourish smoothly and splendidly.

Through the selfless services of the members of the Charity Fund, sufficient land was acquired for this purpose, and a spacious building was erected before long. The members of the fund get together at least twice a year. They have established a funeral aid society and an education fund for students belonging to the Thaliath families. The annual excursions conducted under the auspices of the fund, along with the athletic and literary competitions held among family members, have gone a long way towards strengthening the bonds of love and cooperation among the members of our family.
