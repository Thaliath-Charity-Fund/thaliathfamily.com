Our History

From the Land of Puthenpally

The story of the Thaliath family of Puthenpally, Varapuzha, and of the charity fund our forefathers founded for the unity and welfare of the family.

Puthenpally, Varapuzha

Where the family's roots are found

Puthenpally is part of the famous village of Varapuzha in the district of Ernakulam in Kerala. Once upon a time the village of Varapuzha was the headquarters of the Indian Catholic Church. What is more, the well known Catholic seminary where aspirants for priesthood were prepared was set up in Puthenpally of this village. Hence, hallowed is the land of Puthenpally by the footprints of innumerable holy men of the past.

The nerve centre of the ancient family of Thaliath, so deep rooted in Christian traditions and true faith, can be traced back to this village.

The Thaliath Charity Fund

Founded in 1954

Having thus founded the family on the rock bed of power and pluck, the forefathers of our family formed a charity fund in the year 1954, for the unity and welfare of the entire family and others as well. Each member of the family made a monthly contribution to the fund and raised enough capital for the Thaliath Charity Fund, and the activities of the fund began to flourish in a smooth and splendid manner.

Through the selfless services of the members of the charity fund, sufficient land was acquired for the purpose and a spacious building was erected before long.

The members of the fund get together at least twice a year. They have constituted a funeral aid society and an education fund for the students belonging to the Thaliath families. The annual excursions conducted under the auspices of the fund, and the athletic and literary competitions held among the members of the family, have contributed a long way towards strengthening the love and co-operation among the members of our family.
