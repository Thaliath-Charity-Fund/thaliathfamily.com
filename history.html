Our History

A word about the Thaliath family of Puthenpally, Varapuzha

Puthenpally is part of the famous village of Varapuzha in the Ernakulam district of Kerala. Once upon a time, the village of Varapuzha was the headquarters of the Indian Catholic Church. Moreover, the well-known Catholic seminary, where aspirants to the priesthood were prepared, was established at Puthenpally in this village. Hence, the land of Puthenpally is hallowed by the footprints of innumerable holy men of the past.

The nerve centre of the ancient Thaliath family, so deeply rooted in Christian traditions and true faith, can be traced back to this village. The first native Christian Chief Justice of Travancore in the history of Kerala and the second Surgeon General of Travancore were descendants of the illustrious Thaliath family of Puthenpally. The family also takes pride in having produced the sixth Prior General of the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI), India's first indigenous religious congregation, and the first Bishop of Rajkot.

Furthermore, a doctor who was honoured with the prestigious Padma Shri Award by the Government of India and a Sister who embraced religious life and was accorded the title of Servant of God by the Catholic Church have further enhanced the honour, prestige and reputation of the Thaliath family.

The Thaliath family also had the rare distinction of being represented in the last Legislative Council of Travancore and the first Legislative Assembly of Travancore-Cochin (1949–1951), with one of its members being elected to the Assembly. This historic achievement was indeed a moment of great pride and honour for the entire family.

Having thus founded the family on the rock bed of power and pluck, the forefathers of our family established a charity fund in 1954 for the unity and welfare of the entire family and others as well. Each member of the family made a monthly contribution to the fund, thereby raising sufficient capital for the Thaliath Charity Fund. The activities of the fund then began to flourish smoothly and splendidly.

Through the selfless services of the members of the Charity Fund, sufficient land was acquired for this purpose, and a spacious building was erected before long. The members of the fund get together at least twice a year. They have established a funeral aid society and an education fund for students belonging to the Thaliath families. The annual excursions conducted under the auspices of the fund, along with the athletic and literary competitions held among family members, have gone a long way towards strengthening the bonds of love and cooperation among the members of our family.
